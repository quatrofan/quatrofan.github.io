Privacy Policy

Application: Woodly · Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Quatro Fan ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the mobile game Woodly (the "App"). By using the App you agree to the practices described below. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Who we are

The App is developed and published by Quatro Fan. For any questions about this Policy or about your data, contact us at: quatro.fan.game@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

You do not need to create an account or provide your name, email, or other personal contact details to play. The App collects the following categories of data automatically or through third-party services:

  • Advertising data. We show ads (full-screen ads between levels and optional rewarded video ads) through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may process your device's advertising identifier, IP address, general device information (model, operating system, language), and your interaction with ads.
  • Consent data. If you are asked for consent (see section 4), your choice is stored on your device and processed by Google's User Messaging Platform so that it can be respected by the advertising service.
  • Local data on your device. Your game progress (current level, completed daily puzzles, collected figurines, boosters, stars) and settings (sound, music, vibration, language) are stored only on your device and are not transmitted to us.

We do not collect your real name, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise location (GPS), or payment information. The App does not access your camera or microphone. The App does not use analytics services of its own. The current version of the App contains no in-app purchases.

3. How we use the data

  • To operate the game and remember your progress and settings;
  • To show advertising, which keeps the App free, and to give you in-game rewards for watching optional video ads;
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues or abuse (for example, ad fraud).

4. Legal basis and your consent (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads, using a consent form provided through Google's User Messaging Platform. You can review and change your choice at any time from the App (Settings → Ad settings). Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time; you will then see non-personalized ads. We also process certain data on the basis of our legitimate interest in operating and securing the App.

5. Third-party services

The App relies on the following third-party services, which process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform — https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services — https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

6. Advertising choices

You can opt out of personalized advertising at the device level. On Android, open Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) and delete or reset your advertising ID, or turn on "Opt out of Ads Personalization". Users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland can also change their consent choice at any time in the App.

7. Device permissions

  • Internet and advertising ID. Used only by the advertising service described above. The game itself can be played offline.

8. Children

The App is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Data retention and deletion

We do not create user accounts and do not store your personal data on our own servers. Advertising data is handled by Google according to its policies. Data stored locally on your device (progress and settings) is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings.

10. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, and to data portability. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at quatro.fan.game@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

11. Data security

We rely on the security measures provided by our service provider (Google), including encryption in transit, to protect your data. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. International data transfers

Our service provider may process and store data on servers located outside your country of residence, including in the United States. Where required, such transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards under the provider's privacy frameworks.

13. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this Policy periodically.
